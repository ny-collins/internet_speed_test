Why Test from Amsterdam?

Most speed tests use nearby servers and show idealized local speeds. We test from Amsterdam, Netherlands to show your real-world international internet speed - what you actually experience when streaming European content, gaming on international servers, or accessing global websites.

Learn More About Our Methodology

Download: How fast you receive data from the internet. Measured in Mbps (Megabits per second). Higher is better for streaming, downloading files, and browsing.

Upload: How fast you send data to the internet. Important for video calls, uploading files, and live streaming.

Ping (Latency): Response time delay. Lower is better. Critical for gaming and real-time communication.

Jitter: Variation in latency. Lower is better. High jitter causes choppy video calls and game lag.

Internet data doesn't travel instantly. Signals pass through multiple network hops, undersea cables, and continental routing infrastructure. Physics limits speed - light travels at 300,000 km/s, but internet signals travel slower through fiber optic cables.

The further you are from Europe, the higher your latency and the more network congestion can affect your speed. This test shows your real-world international performance.

Multiple factors impact your results:

  • ISP routing quality: How efficiently your ISP routes traffic to Europe
  • Time of day: Network congestion during peak hours
  • Network equipment: Router quality, WiFi vs wired connection
  • Background usage: Other devices and applications using your network
  • Undersea cable capacity: International link congestion

If other speed tests show higher speeds, they're likely using nearby servers in your region. Both results are valid - they just measure different things:

Local speed tests: Show your maximum network capacity and ISP connection quality.

Our international test: Shows real-world performance when accessing European content, gaming on international servers, or video conferencing abroad.

Think of it like measuring highway speed vs. actual trip time - both are useful metrics for different purposes.

Streaming: 25+ Mbps for 4K, 10+ Mbps for HD, 5+ Mbps for SD

Gaming: <50ms ping excellent, 50-100ms good, >100ms difficult

Video Calls: 5+ Mbps and <100ms ping with low jitter (<10ms)

File Downloads: Higher download speed = faster downloads

Cloud Backup: Higher upload speed = faster backup times

Bufferbloat: Excessive latency caused by packet buffering in network equipment. Our variance graph helps identify this issue.

TCP Window Scaling: How much data can be "in flight" between you and the server. Long distances require larger windows.

Packet Loss: Lost data packets require retransmission, reducing effective speed.

QoS & Traffic Shaping: ISPs may prioritize certain types of traffic, affecting test results.

Results may vary based on network conditions, time of day, and server load. For most accurate results, close other applications using internet.
