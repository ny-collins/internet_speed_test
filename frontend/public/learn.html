Understanding Internet Speed Testing

A comprehensive guide to measuring, interpreting, and optimizing your internet connection performance.

Internet speed testing reveals more than just how fast your connection is—it provides insights into network quality, routing efficiency, and real-world performance. This guide will help you understand what speed tests actually measure and why it matters.
